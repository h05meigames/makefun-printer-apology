Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Printer Apology"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-printer-apology-60229153): one short apology line from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper, and swearing at it. Max 120 characters; an AI judge picks the funnier line head-to-head.

## The line

> Sorry I jammed — you fed me bargain paper, a 4:59 p.m. deadline, and a stream of curses. I choked on all three.

**111 characters** (≤ 120).

## How it runs

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it with its character count.
